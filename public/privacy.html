Privacy Policy

Checkride · last updated 8 September 2026

Checkride does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There is no account to create, nothing to sign in to, and no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

What the app stores

Everything the app remembers is kept on your own device and never leaves it unless you choose to export it. That is:

  • Which checklist items you have ticked
  • Aircraft you have marked as favourites, and which you opened recently
  • Checklist items you add yourself, and notes you write against an aircraft
  • Your settings: theme, text size, sim filter and the toggles

On the web this uses your browser's local storage; in the installed apps it uses the device's own app storage. Deleting the app, or clearing the site data in your browser, deletes all of it permanently.

Exporting your data

The app can write the above to a file so you can back it up or move it to another device. That file is created on your device and goes wherever you choose to put it. It is never uploaded anywhere by the app.

Network access

The installed apps make no network requests. The web version is downloaded from its host like any website, and is then cached so it works offline; no data about you is sent back.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Not affiliated

Checkride is an independent reference tool for flight simulation. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Microsoft, Asobo Studio, or any aircraft manufacturer. Aircraft names are used only to identify the type each checklist belongs to. The checklists are simplified for use in a simulator and must never be used to operate a real aircraft.
